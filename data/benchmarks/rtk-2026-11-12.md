Rejected candidates fall back to raw bytes and count as zero reduction. The
in-process exact references are tailored answers, not a general compressor or
an equivalent latency arm. Filter times in this sample include process startup
and have no repeated-run confidence interval.

The [JSON report](rtk-2026-11-12.json) contains per-task hashes and timings plus
the hash of the runner used for this measurement. The current runner may gain
metadata or reporting changes; the fixture hashes distinguish changed inputs.
The fixture generator is `src/rtk.ts` in this source tree. The original measured
runner and raw/candidate evidence were retained locally during the rebuild.
The per-case reproduction is `bun run ats bench --out DIR` with RTK 1.2.0.

See [interpretation and limits](../../docs/RTK.md). This sample is deliberately
adversarial and is not a general failure rate for RTK. Literal preservation is
weaker than semantic equivalence or a completed real-world task.
