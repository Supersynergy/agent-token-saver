# Measurement archive

`benchmarks/` contains dated reports and usage summaries. These are historical
measurements, not current model recommendations, prices or ATS 5.1 savings claims.
See [the reviewed findings](../docs/BENCHMARK_REVIEW.md) before reusing a number.

Full provider/session JSONL traces are excluded from the maintained release.
The original local evidence was archived before removal. Published summaries
keep their measurements; absent raw traces limit independent reproduction.
Older reports may link to retired commands or trace files. Those links describe
the original experiment and are not current installation instructions.

`research/speed-review-2026-11-12.json` records source hashes for the local
Speed/Superloop review. Hashes identify the reviewed source versions; they do
not make private source documents publicly retrievable or prove the conclusions.

When comparing two archived rows, first confirm that their fixtures, acceptance
conditions and usage classes match; a repeated task name alone does not establish
that they answer the same question or can share a cost denominator.
